Phase 01 — Unlocked
Market Analysis
Understand your audience before building anything. This is the foundation everything else runs on.
Why this matters
Most creators skip this and build for an assumed audience. That's how you end up with a launch that doesn't convert.
01
Know their problem
Your audience has specific pain points. Generic offers don't sell. Problem-specific offers do.
02
Know their outcome
What does success look like for them? Your offer should be the bridge from where they are to where they want to be.
03
Know their budget
What will they actually pay? This determines whether you build a $47 community or a $3,000 mentorship first.
Execution Strategy
Run this for 3–4 days to your existing audience. You collect the data. We build from it.
Step 1 — Tease
Post a story or reel hinting that you're building something new for your audience. Don't reveal details yet. Create curiosity.
Step 2 — Incentivise
Offer a free guide (your roadmap PDF) or a chance to win a 1:1 session. Drive audience to the form link in your bio.
Step 3 — Collect
Your form collects: current situation, core problem, desired outcome, and budget range. This defines your ICP.

Phase 02 — Offer Architecture
How Your Offer Is Built
Every high-converting offer is built from five core elements. Each one will be handled for you — based on the data from Phase 1.
01
Unique Value Proposition
Who it's for. What it solves. What it transforms.
Handled for you

Your UVP defines exactly who you help, the core problem you solve, and the specific transformation they achieve — positioned as the fastest, clearest path from where they are to where they want to be.

Before
Posting consistently, no income, unclear what to sell
→
After
Clear offer, warm audience, consistent monthly revenue
◆ This will be defined using your Phase 1 audience data
02
Pricing Strategy
Positioned by demand, value, and delivery model.
Handled for you

Price is not chosen at random. It reflects the perceived value of the outcome, the delivery format, and what your specific audience is willing to pay — determined by Phase 1 data.

Low Ticket
$27 — $97
Community access, monthly membership
Mid Ticket
$297 — $997
Program, course, cohort
High Ticket
$2k — $10k+
1:1 mentorship, DFY service
◆ Pricing is aligned with value and delivery — not chosen randomly
03
Delivery System
How the transformation is delivered.
Handled for you

Delivery format directly affects perceived value and the results your clients achieve. The right model depends on your offer type and audience expectations.

◎
1:1
Direct, personalised. Highest perceived value.
◈
Group / Community
Recurring, scalable. Peer-driven momentum.
▣
Course (DIY)
Self-paced. High leverage, low overhead.
◇
Hybrid (DWY)
Course + support. Strong completion rates.
✦
Service (DFY)
Full execution. Premium, application-only.
04
Trim & Stack
Remove what weakens it. Add what strengthens it.
Handled for you

Most offers fail not because of the idea — but because they carry too much noise and not enough clarity. Trimming sharpens the core. Stacking increases perceived value without increasing complexity.

Before — Messy Offer
10 modules with unclear outcomes
3 different audiences targeted
Undefined delivery timeline
No clear transformation promise
→
After — Focused Offer
6-week program, one clear outcome
One specific ICP, one problem
Weekly milestones, clear progress
"From X to Y in Z weeks"
05
Product Enhancement
Bonuses. Guarantees. Naming.
Handled for you

The right name, a clear guarantee, and well-placed bonuses can double conversion without changing the core offer. These are applied after the offer structure is locked.

✦
Strong Naming
"The 6-Week Revenue Sprint"
vs "My Online Program"
◆
Guarantee Concept
"Complete all modules. If no result, we work until you do."
◈
Bonuses
Templates, 1:1 call, private community access — added to raise perceived value
This is how your offer will be structured and optimised based on your data.
